Common Questions

Do I need to bring my own equipment? expand_more
We recommend bringing your own paddle, but we do have premium rental paddles and balls available at the pro shop for a small fee.
How far in advance can I book? expand_more
Courts can be booked up to 14 days in advance. Members get early access to book up to 21 days in advance.
What is the cancellation policy? expand_more
Cancellations made 24 hours prior to your booking will receive a full refund. Late cancellations may be subject to a fee.
